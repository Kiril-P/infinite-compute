# Infinite Compute domain

This glossary defines the game language shared by design, code, tests, and agent tickets. It describes concepts only; implementation belongs in specifications and ADRs.

## Progression

**Era**:
A stage of the computer's physical scale, interface complexity, available jobs, and relationship with the outside world.
_Avoid_: Level, world, zone

**Transition**:
A one-time milestone that advances the computer into the next Era and visibly transforms its environment.
_Avoid_: Prestige, reset, ascension

**Impossible Question**:
The unresolved question about existence that motivates the computer's long-term pursuit of infinite computing power.
_Avoid_: Final quest, win condition

**V1 Ending**:
The first data-center supercomputer benchmark and the realization that its Compute is still negligible relative to the Impossible Question.
_Avoid_: Final answer, game completion

## Economy

**Job**:
A bounded piece of work the computer processes to earn a reward or unlock a milestone.
_Avoid_: Quest, order, mission

**Cash**:
The initial spendable resource earned from Jobs and used for commercial hardware, software, and infrastructure.
_Avoid_: Coins, credits, money points

**Research**:
A later spendable resource representing new knowledge and used for breakthroughs, automation, applications, and Transitions.
_Avoid_: Science, knowledge points

**Compute**:
The computer's derived processing capability, used to determine Job speed and eligibility rather than spent as currency in v1.
_Avoid_: Processing currency, FLOPs points

**Bottleneck**:
A visible condition in which memory, temperature, power, or bandwidth reduces effective Compute below raw Compute.
_Avoid_: Debuff, failure

## Interaction

**Active Interaction**:
A short timing or decision activity through which attention earns a temporary advantage without rewarding repeated clicking.
_Avoid_: Clicker, manual grind, minigame

**Automation**:
A system that performs a previously learned repetitive action and establishes reliable baseline progress.
_Avoid_: Autoclicker, idle mode

**Incident**:
A brief, legible disruption with a cause, effect, and recovery that changes efficiency without destroying progress.
_Avoid_: Disaster, random failure

## Machine

**Hardware Category**:
A repeatable upgrade family: CPU, RAM, storage, GPU, cooling, power, or network.
_Avoid_: Building, generator

**Tier Gate**:
A one-time physical or infrastructural upgrade such as a motherboard, chassis, rack, room, or electrical service that raises Hardware Category limits and may trigger a Transition.
_Avoid_: Hardware Category, prestige upgrade

**Software**:
Purchasable or researchable capability that changes automation, global efficiency, Job behavior, or available applications.
_Avoid_: Hardware, spell

**Operating System**:
The interface, narrator, and developing protagonist through which every game system and story beat is presented.
_Avoid_: Assistant, player character, HUD

## Narrative

**Announcement**:
A triggered outside-world report that communicates progression, consequence, or satire at the current Era's scale.
_Avoid_: Dialogue, lore dump

**System Message**:
A short statement from the Operating System about its state, observations, or developing understanding.
_Avoid_: Announcement, narrator caption
