## Slice 1: Research has a physical budget

- Workstation transition needs Memory Pressure passed, CPU 12 / RAM 8 / Storage 4 / GPU 4 / Cooling 3 and $2,500. It keeps all parts, Jobs, pending batch, software, history and automation; adds no minimum time. The verified graphics-first Home route reaches these parts at 21 m 46.3 s, and Research unlocks at 21m49.6s in the verified baseline route.
- The workstation multiplies raw CPU and GPU scales by 10, exposes caps CPU 16 / RAM 12 / Storage 6 / GPU 6 / Cooling 5, and raises subsequent repeatable prices ×10 from their Home values. The whole-phase route evidence below covers these economy choices.
- Research opens immediately, with 5 Research from this once-only milestone. It uses 10,000 delivered work per Research point. Fractional point progress persists; spending/refunds use whole points. Lifetime earned Research is separate from current balance. A first purchase must be reachable within two minutes without a bonus or paid software.
- Named Operating Profiles reserve 25% (Balanced), 10% (Throughput), or 70% (Research) of capacity. Research emphasis keeps a 30% Cash-producing share. CPU and GPU each use the same reserved fraction. A Job receives its share of CPU plus its compatible graphics share (ordinary Jobs 15%, logo/protein 100%). Research receives the remainder, including incompatible unused graphics. During Job setup/manual standby the unused Job allocation is lent to Research. Both allocations sum to no more than available capacity; the UI shows the actual live split and finite delivered output.
- Benchmarks isolate ordinary Jobs and Research; no Research or software renewal advances during an attempt. Forecasts use the same allocation/specialization rules as processing. Hardware/tuning changes affect future processing immediately but cannot reset existing work or repeat a milestone.
- Initial permanent discoveries: Parallel Library (40 Research) unlocks a protein work-unit Job; Queue Compiler (60 Research, requires Library) reduces future Job setup to 60%. Initial refundable tuning: Branch Cache (25 Research, +15% CPU) and Wide Vectors (25 Research, +25% GPU). The tree contains eight meaningful nodes including thermal mastery in slice 2; no mutually exclusive purchase or more than two branch points.
- Protein work unit: 50,000 work, $16,000, 36 MB, 4 s base setup, full CPU+GPU compatibility. The first completion awards 5 Research once, separate from its recurring Cash payout. It is an ordinary modeled work unit, not a claim to solve a real scientific problem.
- Buying tuning records its exact paid price and enables it. Free Recompile Kernel refunds all owned tuning at those paid prices, preserves permanent discoveries and all earned milestones, and clears the current Job bonus opportunity (attempt consumed, bonus zero) before changing effects. Recompile is unavailable during a Benchmark. Profiles never purchase/refund Research.
- Each named profile can save the current queue policy and enabled tuning IDs. Applying a saved profile restores the policy and only owned tuning; unavailable saved selections remain recorded with an explanation. A fresh profile preserves the current tuning selection. Rebuying a refunded selection is an explicit purchase, never a profile side effect.
- Schema 7 adds workstation/Research state to older saves with defaults. Migration must preserve schema 6 history, software, Jobs and policies. The workstation environment and Research Lab are code-authored, respond to snapshots, and fit the same no-scroll shell.

## Slice 2 implementation contract — verified, 2026-09-08

Teach modeled power and temperature in ordinary Workstation play before the Benchmark. This is a game model, not a physical engineering simulator.

- Workstation supply starts at 450 W. Power upgrades add 150 W, cap 2, cost ceil(90,000×1.18^level). Active modeled draw is 80 +12×CPU +50×GPU +8×Cooling watts. Eco uses 80% raw clock and 65% draw; Standard uses 100%. A Thermal Window uses 125% raw clock and 140% draw. Power efficiency is min(1,supply/draw), floored at 50%; show supply, demand and exact factor together.
- Temperature starts 30°C. Each 100 ms approaches the current equilibrium by 1/200 of the difference (20-second response). Equilibrium is 30 +5×draw/(30+10×effectiveCooling). Cooling also retains its existing GPU airflow effect. Warning begins 60°C; thermal penalty begins 70°C and linearly falls to 50% by 90°C. Effective CPU/GPU receive memory, airflow, power and thermal factors once. Eco/power/cooling remain clear recovery paths; Cash cannot become negative and hardware is never damaged.
- Four additional Research nodes complete the eight-node configuration: Thermal Model (permanent 70 R, requires Parallel Library) unlocks Thermal Window and Heat Soak; Open Interconnect (permanent 100 R, requires Queue Compiler) reduces modeled demand 15%; Efficient Pages (refundable 35 R) multiplies memory efficiency 1.1, capped 100%; Research Notebook (refundable 50 R, requires Thermal Model) earns 20% more Research per allocated work. Existing owners/prerequisites are preserved. Conversion efficiency does not create extra assigned capacity.
- Saved profiles additionally capture Eco/Standard clock intent. Old saved profiles migrate to Standard. Every operation still guards Benchmark isolation.
- Thermal Window is optional, once per 60 s of ordinary foreground time. Starting requires Thermal Model and 55–72°C. Timing mode offers two broad adjustments at 3–6 s and 8–11 s during a 15 s boost; each accepts one input and lowers temperature 4°C. Early/late/repeated/missed adjustment ends only the boost; no Job/Cash/Research is erased. Recompile ends an active window. The Research split still applies to boosted finite capacity.
- Untimed decision mode reserves the opportunity without a running bonus deadline. At starting temperature below 64°C choose the Steady curve; otherwise choose Pre-cool. The prompt states this rule and current temperature. A correct plan schedules the same two cooling adjustments automatically during the 15 s boost; an incorrect plan ends only this opportunity. The 60 s cooldown starts when the opportunity is committed, never bypassed by switching modes/profiles. Ordinary Jobs continue while choosing.
- Heat Soak prerequisites: Thermal Model, an observed ordinary warm state (60°C), CPU 14/RAM 10/Cooling 4/Power 1. Its sole altered capacity rule fixes effective Cooling at 2. The screen previews Eco and Standard temperature/output. Deliver 1,200,000 work and finish at or below 70°C. Eco is the free safe configuration; Standard can overheat and fail, or succeed with sufficient permanent efficiency. No fee. Preserve/pause the ordinary Job, Research generation, software renewal, ordinary temperature and optional window/cooldown. Isolated attempt temperature/work/strategy/result survive reload. At the work goal, a configuration whose equilibrium exceeds 70°C stops overheated with an explicit exit/change/retry path. A safe configuration holds the test load while cooling to 70°C and then certifies; this handles hot starts and faster tuned machines without a false failure. Failure consumes no progress outside the attempt.
- Success awards the permanent automatic fan curve once (+15 to ordinary cooling denominator) and 10 milestone Research. Ordinary modifiers/temperature return immediately; no attempt-temperature leakage. Fan curve is not refundable. No entry/exit/reload can repeat rewards.
- Schema 8 adds operations defaults to old saves and saved profiles. Strict validation covers clock, power, temperature, thermal-window timing/attempt ownership and isolated Benchmark results, preserving old Research conservation/history. Targeted tests must verify mixed factors, no double allocation, tuning conservation, non-destructive failures, decision/timing controls, paused ordinary state, retry/reload and one-time reward.
- UI slice: Task Manager gains pages for queue/capacity, operations and Heat Soak; Hardware gains an actual Power purchase; Job Runner replaces the mastered batch interaction with Thermal Window; contextual Help explains safe recovery. Show one focused page at a time, preserve the no-scroll shell, bind fan/activity/temperature visuals to snapshots and add distinct opt-in Workstation cues. Required progress does not require a Thermal Window.
