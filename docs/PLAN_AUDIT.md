# Planning audit and revision

Date: 2026-09-07. Scope: audit the existing plan, strengthen the user's intended satire and Quest for Meaning, and plan the complete campaign. Documentation only; no implementation was started.

Historical planning snapshot: the pause and absence-of-runtime statements below describe this audit, before the user subsequently authorized Phase 0. [Continuity](CONTINUITY.md) owns current execution and verification; the planning conclusions remain preserved here.

## Finding

The original plan was strongest at the v1 progression/React seam and weakest at explaining what the game was ultimately about. The opening and ending specifications made the Impossible Question an almost absent sequel hook. The later game had a list of possible scales without loops, resource handoffs, distinct decisions, or an ending the player could perform.

The revised product makes the pursuit of meaning present from the opening, gives satire recurring institutional targets and mechanical consequences, and plans a finite campaign through the Final Answer and its aftermath. The same OS, useful work, and memories survive every scale change.

## What the full-game design adds

- Planetary Jobs teach locality, serial work, bandwidth, and coordination through visible forecasts.
- Orbital and stellar construction introduces Fabrication once, reusing power/heat through collection and radiator decisions.
- Interstellar regions delegate work around delayed messages; cosmic verification separates matching outputs from matching assumptions.
- The final finite calculation returns 42, exposes the inherited request's missing purpose, and uses familiar Job controls for a chosen useful epilogue.
- Later growth preserves the original machine and its memories; no required prestige or new save is needed.
